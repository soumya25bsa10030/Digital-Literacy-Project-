I am Soumya Tandon of branch BTECH in Computer Science (Cloud Computing and Automation) and I enrolled in the course in year 2025 . My goals include mastering coding skills in Python and C++ and learn skills that help me solve problems on the ground level . I created this project as part of my role as a Student Digital Ambassador to help my peers navigate the digital world safely, responsibly, and professionally. This repository contains work from five modules of the course, each designed to develop a different aspect of digital literacy.

## Module 1 – Digital Literacy Awareness: 
I designed a one-page infographic using [Canva] to explain what digital literacy is, highlight useful tools for students, and share safe internet practices. This visual resource aims to make digital literacy easy to understand for my batchmates.

## Module 2 – Professional Online Presence:
I set up accounts on GitHub, LinkedIn, and Hacker Rank  to build my professional digital identity. My GitHub profile README introduces me, my branch, year, and learning goals. Screenshots of all platforms show how I plan to use them academically and professionally over the next few years.

## Module 3 – Coding & Collaboration Platforms:
I practiced coding on HackerRank by completing a beginner-level challenge and created a Google Form quiz on digital literacy for peer engagement. Screenshots and links demonstrate my work and how these platforms enhance both learning and collaboration.

## Module 4 – Professional Communication & Social Media: 
I drafted two professional emails — one to a professor and another to an internship coordinator — and created a social media do’s and don’ts checklist. This module reinforced proper online communication and etiquette.

## Module 5 – Cybercrime Awareness:
I researched a cybercrime scenario (e.g., phishing), wrote a detailed case study, and created a “Stay Safe Online” checklist with actionable tips for students, including financial safety and reporting channels.

This portfolio not only showcases my practical digital skills but also serves as a guide for peers to safely and responsibly use online tools, build professional profiles, and protect themselves from cyber threats
