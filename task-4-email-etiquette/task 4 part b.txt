> Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

* Do's
1. Think before posting – Misinformation or offensive messages should not be spread so check before posting.
2. Use privacy settings – Adjust your accounts to control who sees your content and protect your passwords.
3. Engage positively – Share supportive comments, constructive feedback, and helpful resources.
4. Verify information – Check facts before sharing news or articles.
5. Separate personal and professional content – Keep your academic or career-related posts professional.





* Don'ts
1. Post inappropriate content – Avoid offensive, discriminatory, or harmful material.
2. Personal information – Do not share sensitive details like your address, passwords or financial information.
3. Engage in online arguments – Avoid heated disputes that could escalate or harm your image.
4. Ignore copyright laws – Do not share music, videos, or images without proper permission.
5. Follow unreliable sources blindly – Avoid spreading misinformation or fake news.
