TASK 5 : Case study writeup, prevention checklist
1) Have a different password for every internet account. DO NOT USE THE SAME PASSWORD on multiple of your accounts.

2) Use 2FA (Two Factor Authentication) on EVERY account – email, banking, social media, etc.

3) Do not share your One Time Password, your User ID, Password or any other personal information with anyone, even if the person claims to be calling from a bank or an official service.

4) Be cautious about links, emails, and messages, especially when someone asks you to respond quickly or offers some kind of reward for doing so.

5) Regularly update software (security patches) on your computer, smartphone, etc. – Doing so reduces the chance of being a victim of a cybercrime due to a technology vulnerability.

6) Do not use Public Wi-Fi to access Sensitive Internet Accounts (such as your banking account or checking email).

7) If you are using UPI Apps (Unified Payment Interface Apps) – Always double check that you are sending money the right person.

8) Do not approve any “collect requests,” or pay any “collect request” from someone you know using UPI Apps.

9) If you are going to download Apps, always look for a well-known Developed name to make sure that the App you are looking at is from a trusted/legitimate Developer.
